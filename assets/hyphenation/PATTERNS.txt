under the MIT, BSD-3-Clause, Unlicense or FSFAP-no-warranty-disclaimer licences. Where a
file offers several licences, ferri-pdf uses it under the MIT licence. The patterns are
compiled into hypher's automata unmodified. The copyright and licence notice of each
pattern file follows.
